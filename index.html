Frequently asked questions

What does z88 Technologies do?

z88 builds you a personal portfolio website from your resume, social links and photos. AI writes the copy and designs the site, and it is published at yourname.z88.tech.

What do I need to get started?

Only a handle and your name. A resume (PDF, DOCX or TXT), up to 10 portfolio images, a short bio and your social links are optional and help the AI write better copy.

How long does it take?

About 2 to 5 minutes. You see live progress while the copy is written, the site is built and deployed, and your subdomain and HTTPS certificate are connected.

Where will my site be hosted?

Your site is hosted for you at your own subdomain, yourname.z88.tech, served over HTTPS. You do not need your own hosting or domain.

What happens to the files I upload?

Your resume and details are used to write your site. Images you upload are published on your site. Temporary copies are deleted from our server once your site is built. See the privacy policy for details.
