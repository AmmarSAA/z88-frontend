Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information z88 Technologies ("z88", "we") collects when you use z88.tech to generate a portfolio website, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email contact@z88.tech.

What we collect

  • Details you enter: your handle, name, and optionally your contact email, tagline, bio and social profile links.
  • Files you upload: a resume (PDF, DOCX or TXT) and up to 10 images.
  • Technical data: your IP address and basic request details, which our API uses to limit abuse and keep the service running.
  • Contact form messages: if someone uses the contact form on a generated site, their name, email address and message.

The z88.tech website does not use advertising or analytics trackers and does not set tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To write the copy for your site, using an AI model that reads your details, your resume text and public information about the social links you provide.
  • To build, publish and host your site at yourname.z88.tech.
  • To forward contact form messages from your site to the email address you gave us.
  • To protect the service against abuse, for example through rate limiting.

What is published

Your generated site is public. It shows your name, the AI-written copy, the images you uploaded, your social links and, if you chose a contact option, a way for visitors to reach you. Please only upload content you have the right to publish. Your resume file itself is not published.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the service. Each processes data only as needed for its role:

  • Groq: runs the AI model that writes your site copy.
  • Google Custom Search: looks up public information about the social links you provide.
  • GitHub: stores your site's source code in a private repository.
  • Netlify: builds and hosts your site and this website.
  • Cloudflare: provides DNS for your subdomain.
  • Render: hosts the z88 API.
  • Google (Gmail): delivers contact form emails.

How long we keep it

  • Uploaded files are stored temporarily while your site is built and are then deleted from our server. Images you uploaded remain part of your published site.
  • Generation progress records are kept for up to 24 hours.
  • Your site and its source code are kept until you ask us to remove them.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your site and the information behind it, or ask what information we hold about you, by emailing contact@z88.tech from the address you used, or with your handle. We will respond within 30 days.

Security

All traffic to z88.tech, the API and generated sites is encrypted with HTTPS. To report a security issue, email contact@z88.tech.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
